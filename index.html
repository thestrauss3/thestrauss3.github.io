About

I’m a full-stack software engineer based in San Diego. I spent nearly nine years at Buildium, building Angular interfaces and C#/.NET services for property managers and residents. I like working across the stack, whether I’m building features that work seamlessly across Angular and AngularJS or tracing a slow request from the browser back to the database.

At Buildium, I used OpenSearch to turn complex database queries into fast, scalable search. I became a go-to engineer for OpenSearch and Elasticsearch, helping teams across the company design new search features and writing guides they could use to extend search on their own.

I served as a Cyber Network Operator in the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve, where I maintained secure networks, troubleshot communications in the field, and trained fellow Marines. My time in the Marines taught me to stay calm when things go wrong, take responsibility for seeing things through, and share what I know so the people around me can do their jobs better.
