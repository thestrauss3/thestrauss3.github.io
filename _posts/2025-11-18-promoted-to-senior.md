Eight years ago, I was lucky enough to start my journey as a software engineering intern at Buildium, a place that’s shaped both my career and who I am. Today, I’m proud to share that I’ve been promoted to Senior Software Engineer.

This past month has been a whirlwind. Between becoming a dad and stepping into this new role, it’s been a season of learning and perspective.

Over the past year, I joined the team behind Buildium's new Workflows automation, a massive initiative transforming how property managers handle recurring tasks. I’ve had the opportunity to dive deeper into backend development and large-scale systems work, from building core workflow logic to defining workflow step orchestration to running proof-of-concepts for new architectural approaches.

It’s been a large shift from my earlier front-end-heavy projects like our docked composer, global search functionality, and even the redesigned Resident Center. That transition reminded me why I love engineering. Seeing how the right technical decisions ripple out to make people’s work easier and more reliable. There’s something deeply satisfying about building features people actively use, where the impact is immediate and visible rather than buried behind the scenes.

I’m grateful for all the mentors and teammates who’ve helped me grow along the way, and I’m excited to keep pushing my skills further — learning, solving, and awaiting what comes next.
