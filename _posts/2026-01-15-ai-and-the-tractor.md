Many people are fearful that AI will take over their job.

That fear is understandable. But the reality is that AI is far more likely to change what it means to be effective at your job.

Computers, the internet, and smartphones all triggered similar fears. But the most useful comparison isn’t a modern or futuristic technology. It’s the tractor.

The tractor didn’t eliminate farming. It eliminated certain ways of farming. Farmers who learned to use it became dramatically more productive. Those who didn’t were eventually left behind, not because farming disappeared, but because the work changed.

AI has a similar effect. It doesn’t replace people by default. It shifts value away from repetitive, mechanical tasks and toward judgment, problem framing, and deciding what actually matters.

The people who treat AI like a tool gain leverage. The people who ignore it feel like the rug is being pulled out from under them. This isn’t really about AI. It’s about adapting as the nature of work changes.
